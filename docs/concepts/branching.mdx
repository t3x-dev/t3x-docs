# Branching

Branches let you explore alternative semantic states without losing the stable
line of work.

```text
main:      commit_1 -> commit_2
                         \
experiment:               commit_3 -> commit_4
```

Use branches when you want to compare possible interpretations, product
directions, prompt versions, or document revisions before merging the useful
knowledge back.

## Branches are semantic

In T3X, branches point at commits of meaning. They are not separate copies of
raw text; they are alternate reviewed semantic histories.
