# Introduction

**T3X is Git for meaning.** It turns unstructured material such as prompts,
meeting notes, transcripts, specs, and conversations into structured knowledge
that can be reviewed, committed, diffed, and reused.

## Public alpha surface

The v0.3.0 public alpha surface is intentionally small and currently publish
pending:

| Package | Version | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `@t3x-dev/local` | `0.3.0` | Running the packaged local T3X experience and no-key demo. |
| `@t3x-dev/yops` | `0.3.0` | Applying deterministic YAML operations inside your own app. |

Other packages in the repository may be useful internally, but they are not part
of the current public contract.

## Preview surfaces

CLI, MCP, API, and runner workflows are documented as previews. They are useful
for evaluation and integration work, but they are not release-committed public
surfaces yet.

## How T3X works

T3X follows a reviewable pipeline:

```text
Source -> Meaning -> Commit -> Leaf
```

- **Source** keeps the original material visible.
- **Meaning** extracts structured facts, decisions, risks, and relations.
- **Commit** records reviewed knowledge in an immutable semantic snapshot.
- **Leaf** turns committed knowledge into reusable outputs.

The mutation layer is deterministic. LLMs can propose YOps YAML, but the engine
validates and applies those operations without relying on an LLM.
